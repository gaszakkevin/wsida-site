Privacy Policy

What Should I Dream About? · Last updated: August 2026

What Should I Dream About? ("we," "us," "our") operates the website whatshouldidreamabout.com, the YouTube channel of the same name, and related printable coloring-page and coloring-book products. We make calming sleep videos and activities for children and families. This policy explains what data we handle and how.

Our website

Our website is a static informational site. We do not set tracking or advertising cookies or maintain visitor accounts. The only personal information we collect is what a parent or guardian (18+) chooses to submit in our optional email signup form. Outbound links to YouTube, Pinterest, and Amazon are governed by those services' own privacy policies.

Our content-automation tool and Google user data

We operate a private, single-user automation tool that we (the channel owner) use to produce and publish our own content. Using Google OAuth, this tool accesses only the following, all belonging to our own accounts:

  • Google Sheets — to read and write our own production spreadsheet (the video queue, titles, and descriptions).
  • YouTube Data & Analytics APIs — to upload our own videos, organize them into our own playlists, and read our own channel's analytics.

This tool is operated solely by the channel owner, acting on the channel's own Google account. It does not access, collect, store, or share any data belonging to viewers or other third parties.

How we use and share Google user data

Information obtained through Google APIs is used only to operate our own channel and website. We do not sell it, share it, or transfer it to any third party, and we do not use it for advertising or any purpose unrelated to running our channel. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer and are not shared.

Parent email list

If you are an adult parent or guardian and sign up, we collect only your email address and, optionally, your first name. The form is processed by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) and delivered to us by email. We use it only to send news about new videos, free coloring pages and bedtime tips, never sell it, and you can unsubscribe anytime by replying "unsubscribe" or using the link in any email.

Children's privacy

Our content is intended to be viewed by children under adult supervision. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Our signup form is for adults 18+ only and asks for no information about children.
